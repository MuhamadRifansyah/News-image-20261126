One newspaper front page that sums up Thursday, 26 November 2026 — a Thanksgiving Day in the United States, so the loudest news came from elsewhere. The masthead reads "THE WORLD IN ONE DAY". Fourteen stories get their own panel with a picture and a headline, a numbered world map pins the story locations, a briefs column covers the rest of the day, and a date-check box notes what was checked and what was left out. Every story below was reported on 26 November 2026; nothing was invented, and no story from Indonesia is included.
